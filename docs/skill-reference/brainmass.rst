brainmass
=========

Complete skill: :skill-source:`source <skills/brainmass/SKILL.md>`

Purpose and boundary
--------------------

Use ``brainmass`` for aggregate neural activity at local-circuit, population, region, multi-region, or whole-brain scales. It owns neural-mass models and their orchestration; use ``brainpy-state`` for explicit point neurons and ``braincell`` for explicit cellular mechanisms.

Major contents
--------------

- Select and configure neural-mass models from the model library.
- Couple populations and regions through structured networks.
- Run simulations with inputs, initialization, monitors, sampling, and explicit trial or model axes.
- Add stochastic processes and forward observation models.
- Load datasets and preprocess data for fitting or comparison.
- Sweep parameters, map regimes, fit objectives, and train cognitive tasks.
- Analyze and visualize signals, networks, parameter surfaces, and measured outcomes.
- Batch or compile workloads only when the high-level simulator cannot express the requirement.
