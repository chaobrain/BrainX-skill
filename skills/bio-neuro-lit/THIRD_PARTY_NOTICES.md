## ARIS research literature workflow and helpers

The `bio-neuro-lit` workflow is derived from `skills/research-lit/SKILL.md`, and the helper scripts are adapted from `tools/exa_search.py` and `tools/deepxiv_fetch.py`, in:

- Project: `wanshuiyin/Auto-claude-code-research-in-sleep`
- Upstream commit: `e59008d7a42eea50a2797e55dd0d85bbbf6572f5`
- Copyright: Copyright (c) 2026 wanshuiyin
- License: MIT

The adaptation removes ARIS-specific routing, local-library integrations, generic computer-science discovery sources, and unrelated helper commands. It assigns Europe PMC to primary discovery, the BrainX Full-Text Resolver to default reading, Exa to optional recall expansion, and DeepXiv to optional progressive reading.
