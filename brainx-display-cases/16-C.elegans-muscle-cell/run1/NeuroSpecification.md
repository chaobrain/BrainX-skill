Predictive consistency requires held-out spike count error <= 1 for at least two of three traces, first-spike latency error <= 15 ms for at least two of three, and the correct monotone trend (nondecreasing spike count and nonincreasing first-spike latency as current rises). - Required baselines and controls: Passive leak-only cell under the same protocol; no-stimulus quiet-baseline check; nominal/boundary parameter mechanics checks; exact-pipeline synthetic recovery; three deterministic optimizer starts.
