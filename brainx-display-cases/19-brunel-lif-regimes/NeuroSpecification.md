# NeuroSpecification

- Status: locked
- Researcher approval: The 2026-08-29 request fixes the model and four conditions; the matching committed case-17 contract supplies its previously approved numerical protocol unchanged: 0.1 ms timestep, 500 ms burn-in, 2,000 ms analysis, and five fixed seeds.

## Researcher request
- Brain-modeling question or behavior: Determine whether four Brunel current-based LIF parameter points respectively exhibit synchronous regular, fast synchronous irregular, asynchronous irregular, and slow synchronous irregular activity.
- Requested model, experiment, or comparison: Simulate 10,000 excitatory and 2,500 inhibitory neurons at `(g, eta) = (3, 2), (6, 4), (5, 2), (4.5, 0.9)` over one fixed seed set and compare their measured population states.
- Execution mode: forward-simulation
- Required outputs: One four-condition figure with 0.1 ms instantaneous global rate and its temporal mean above a raster from one fixed random 50-neuron sample; separate PNGs for E/I rates, E/I ISI CV, and global-rate spectra; per-seed metrics and aggregate dominant frequency; exact artifact paths.
- Constraints: Use `NE=10000`, `NI=2500`, exact `CE=1000`, `CI=250`, `Cext=1000`, `tau_m=20 ms`, `t_ref=2 ms`, `theta=20 mV`, `V_reset=10 mV`, excitatory/external jump `JE=0.1 mV`, inhibitory jump `-g JE`, delay `1.5 ms`, and independent external Poisson drive `nu_ext=eta*theta/(JE*CE*tau_m)`. Use BrainPy-State point neurons, BrainEvent exact fixed fan-in communication, BrainUnit quantities, and BrainState execution.

## Inspected data contract
- Data sources and inspected contents: No empirical data were supplied. A matching committed case-17 specification and study record were inspected read-only to recover the approved run duration, seeds, and prospective criteria; deleted worktree artifacts remain untouched.
- Shapes, axes, sampling/time base, and physical units: Each run has 12,500 neurons ordered E then I. Use `dt=0.1 ms`, a 500 ms burn-in, and a 2,000 ms analysis window. The 1.5 ms delay is 15 steps. Store global, E, and I counts on the 0.1 ms analysis grid and spikes for one fixed random sample of 50 total neurons, selected once and reused across all conditions and seeds.
- Required preprocessing and the subset used to fit each transform: Discard burn-in before metrics. Convert count bins to Hz by population size and bin width. Compute per-neuron ISI CV only for neurons with at least four analyzed spikes. Mean-center global rate before Welch estimation; use a Hann window, 8192-sample segments, 50% overlap, constant detrending, density scaling, and a 1-1000 Hz non-DC search range. No transform is fit and no training occurs.
- Mapping from data to model inputs, targets, and observables: Every neuron receives an independent aggregate Poisson count with mean `Cext*nu_ext*dt=eta` per step. Delayed E and I events generate signed voltage jumps. Observables are 0.1 ms E/I/global rates, sampled rasters, E/I firing rates, E/I per-neuron ISI CV distributions and summaries, Welch power spectra, dominant non-DC frequency, spectral peak-to-background ratio, and 1 ms global-rate CV.
- Known data limitations or unresolved mismatches: Aggregate per-neuron external Poisson counts reproduce the summed law of 1,000 independent external afferents without instantiating them. A 2 s window limits low-frequency resolution. ISI CV excludes neurons with fewer than four analyzed spikes. Fixed-size networks can retain weak spectral structure in an asynchronous state.

## Acceptance boundary
- Evidence required for success, failure, or an inconclusive result: Freeze criteria before production. Regular requires pooled E/I mean ISI CV `<0.5`; irregular requires `>=0.7`. Synchrony requires both 1 ms global-rate CV `>=0.2` and dominant peak/background power `>=5`. Fast SI additionally requires dominant frequency 100-300 Hz and mean neuron rate below it; slow SI requires 10-60 Hz and mean neuron rate below it. AI requires irregularity and failure of the complete synchrony predicate. Intermediate CVs or synchronous peaks outside the bands are inconclusive. A requested label is robust only when at least four of five repeats and the aggregate-median metrics satisfy it.
- Required baselines and controls: Verify exact fan-in, unique in-range sources, no autapses, correct signs, 15-step impulse delay, `nu_threshold=10 Hz`, independent reset, deterministic replay, finite State/output, a no-recurrence external-drive smoke, eager/compiled parity, fixed graph reuse within a seed, and fixed raster sample reuse across all outputs.
- Invalid-result conditions: Variable indegree, duplicate fixed-fan-in edges, autapses, wrong source counts, State or RNG carryover between independent conditions, external drive missing the factor `Cext`, unit mismatch, omitted burn-in, thresholds changed after observing outcomes, missing seeds or conditions, non-finite metrics, or regime assignment from frequency alone.
- Allowed claims and explicit non-claims: Report whether each sampled point robustly satisfies the frozen finite-network predicates and list all measured dominant frequencies. Do not claim a continuous phase boundary, exact random-number parity with Brunel (2000), biological realism, or absence of finite-size spectral structure in AI.
